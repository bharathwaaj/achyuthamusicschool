## A heading that matches a follow-up question

Explain using the teacher's own experience. Name the instrument, explain any
Carnatic term the first time it appears, and do not invent facts.

## What to do next

End with a short, friendly next step, such as messaging the school on
WhatsApp or reading about the related class.
