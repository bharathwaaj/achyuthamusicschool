# Blog topics

Planned posts for the blog. Each one answers a real question that parents
or learners type into a search engine. The facts and experience must come
from the school's teachers; nothing here should be invented.

## How a post gets written

1. Ask the teacher the questions listed under the topic. Written answers, a
   WhatsApp voice note or a quick call are all fine.
2. Draft the post from those answers in `src/content/blog/`, starting from
   `_template.md`, with `draft: true`. Follow the voice and copy guidelines
   in `AGENTS.md`.
3. The teacher reads the draft and corrects anything that is not how they
   teach.
4. Set `draft: false` and push. The post, the blog page, the footer link
   and the class page's Read more list appear on the next deploy.

One or two posts a month is plenty. A few good answers do more than many
thin ones.

## Topics, in suggested order

### 1. What age can a child start learning the veena?

- **Search it answers:** "veena classes for kids age", "right age to learn
  veena"
- **For:** parents of young children
- **Related class:** veena
- **Questions for the teacher:**
  - What is the youngest age you usually start a child on the veena, and
    why?
  - What signs tell you a child is ready, or should wait a year?
  - How long are lessons for young children, and how are they different
    from lessons for older students?
  - Can a small child manage a full-size veena, or is a smaller one used?
  - What can parents do at home to help?

### 2. Can you really learn the veena online?

- **Search it answers:** "learn veena online", "online veena classes how
  it works"
- **For:** learners outside Coimbatore, and parents unsure about online
  lessons
- **Related class:** veena
- **Questions for the teacher:**
  - How does an online lesson run, from start to finish?
  - What does a student need: device, camera position, internet speed,
    room setup?
  - How do you check posture and finger position over video?
  - How is tuning handled when the student is at home?
  - What do online students find easy, and what takes extra care?

### 3. How long does it take to learn the veena?

- **Search it answers:** "how long to learn veena", "veena learning time"
- **For:** adults and parents planning ahead
- **Related class:** veena
- **Questions for the teacher:**
  - What does a student usually play after three months, a year, and a few
    years?
  - What makes some students progress faster than others?
  - How much practice do you suggest each week?
  - When do students usually start playing full songs (kritis)?

### 4. Choosing a first veena

- **Search it answers:** "how to buy a veena", "Saraswati veena for
  beginners", "ekandam veena"
- **For:** families about to buy an instrument
- **Related class:** veena
- **Questions for the teacher:**
  - What should a beginner look for in a first veena?
  - What is the difference between an ekandam (single-piece) veena and a
    joined one, and does it matter for a beginner?
  - Are there sizes or features you advise against for beginners?
  - Do you help students choose an instrument? Is it fine to start on a
    borrowed or second-hand one?
- **Note:** avoid naming shops, makers or prices unless the school is
  happy to recommend them publicly.

### 5. How much should my child practise each day?

- **Search it answers:** "how much to practise music daily child",
  "veena practice routine"
- **For:** parents of current and new students
- **Related class:** veena
- **Questions for the teacher:**
  - How many minutes a day do you suggest at each stage?
  - What should a short daily practice include?
  - How do you keep children interested when practice feels like a chore?
  - What is the most common practice mistake you see?
- **Note:** ties in with the #TenMinutesADay idea on the home page, if the
  teacher agrees with it.

### 6. What are sangathis? Our guru's way of teaching

- **Search it answers:** "what is sangathi in Carnatic music"
- **For:** anyone curious about Carnatic music, and families choosing a
  school
- **Related class:** vocal (also suits veena and violin)
- **Questions for the teacher:**
  - How would you explain a sangathi to someone new to Carnatic music?
  - Can you give one well-known song and describe how its sangathis
    develop?
  - How did Sri S.V. Achyutha Iyer teach sangathis? Any memory or habit of
    his that students should know?
  - How does that approach show up in your lessons today?
- **Note:** this is the most distinctive topic the school can write about;
  no other site can tell this story.

### 7. Carnatic violin and Western violin: what changes?

### 8. Starting Carnatic vocal as an adult

- **Search it answers:** "learn Carnatic music as an adult", "Carnatic
  vocal classes for beginners online"
- **For:** adults who always wanted to sing
- **Related class:** vocal
- **Questions for the teacher:**

## Topics to avoid

- One page per city or neighbourhood ("veena classes in Chennai"). Search
  engines treat these as doorway pages.
- General "benefits of music" articles with no teacher experience in them.
- Health or exam-result claims the school cannot back up.
